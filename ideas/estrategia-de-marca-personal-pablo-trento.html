PLAN DE CRISIS Y CONTINGENCIAS

CHALLENGES POTENCIALES

Falta de tiempo para contenido:

  • Batch content creation los domingos
  • Repurpose content en múltiples formatos
  • Delegate design y editing
  • Use templates y automation

Low engagement inicial:

  • Focus en quality over quantity
  • Engage más activamente con otros
  • Join conversations, don't just broadcast
  • Patience y consistency

Impostor syndrome:

  • Document all wins y success stories
  • Seek feedback regular de clients
  • Focus en helping others, not self-promotion
  • Join supportive communities

SUCCESS MULTIPLIERS

Si momentum builds faster:

  • Scale content creation
  • Consider podcast o video series
  • Develop online course o workshop
  • Explore speaking circuit más aggressively
